What these labels do—and do not—mean

The frozen registry rules include healthy-volunteer and interaction-probe studies. Acetaminophen (one top-50 path hit) appears in NCT02305017, a healthy-volunteer interaction study. Caffeine (the popularity hit) appears in NCT03970798 as an index substrate. These labels do not establish testing for benefit in Parkinson patients. Labels and scoring were not changed after viewing the results.

Trial participation does not establish efficacy. The sample is small, labels are incomplete, and this is a retrospective graph holdout, not a temporal test. Secondary endpoints cannot replace the primary result.
