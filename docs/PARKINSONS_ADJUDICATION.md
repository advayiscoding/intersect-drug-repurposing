# Parkinson strict-label adjudication

Research hypothesis, not medical advice. This tool does not tell anyone what to take. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist about any medicine.

These judgments apply the frozen `docs/STRICT_LABEL_RULE.md` at commit `c041084` to every occurrence in `artifacts/label_audit/parkinsons_sources.json`. The source bundle consists of the previously eligible raw drug–trial–intervention records; I applied the additional affirmative disease-population and focal-drug clinical-role gates independently to each occurrence. The machine-readable decisions retain exact registry spans and field paths for each judgment. A clinical symptom effect in diagnosed Parkinson disease qualifies under this rule; neither enrollment in a trial nor inclusion here establishes efficacy, disease modification, or a treatment recommendation.

## Records needing particular attention

- **Topiramate, NCT00794313 — include.** The detailed description says participants receive “placebo, Amantadine 300 mg, Amantadine 300 mg plus Topiramate 150 mg.” The amantadine versus amantadine-plus-topiramate arms give a separable add-on contrast for topiramate, and the secondary modified involuntary movement scale measures dyskinesia. Oral levodopa is background treatment, rather than the focal candidate.
- **Buspirone, NCT02589340 — include.** The eligibility criteria require “Parkinson's disease diagnosis” and current stable amantadine. The stated objective evaluates buspirone *in combination with* amantadine for levodopa-induced dyskinesia; the randomized arms vary buspirone versus placebo. The stable amantadine is background therapy, while buspirone has the clinical contrast.
- **Cannabidiol, NCT02818777 — include.** The brief summary identifies dose safety as the major purpose, but the detailed description explicitly states a “Secondary Specific Aim: To examine the effect of CBD on severity \& duration of tremor and other conditions that are problematic in PD.” The registered MDS-UPDRS total-score outcome provides the clinical measure. The frozen rule permits an explicitly studied clinical secondary objective.
- **Mirabegron, NCT02536976 — include.** Eligibility requires a clinical PD diagnosis. The active arm is mirabegron versus matching placebo, the summary frames mirabegron as treatment for the cognition and bladder-symptom concerns in PD, and the primary measure is change in Montreal Cognitive Assessment score. This is a symptom/cognition study, without an inference about disease modification.
- **Erythromycin, NCT02005029 — include.** The brief summary explicitly asks whether erythromycin with levodopa improves “levodopa absorption and motor function.” The registered motor-performance secondary outcomes are clinical, so the trial extends beyond an absorption-only or PK-only question. Erythromycin is the varied treatment; levodopa is background medication.
- **Rifaximin, NCT01662791 — include.** The study has an observational case-control component, but its detailed description separately offers the Parkinson weight-loss case group open-label rifaximin. The objective proposes determining response to treatment, and weight change after treatment and longitudinal symptoms/quality of life are registered outcomes. No comparative efficacy is inferred from this design.
- **Fesoterodine, NCT02385500 — include.** The eligibility list does not itself say PD, but the brief summary explicitly says “patients with baseline overactive bladder (OAB) symptoms and Parkinson's disease will be recruited” to receive active drug or placebo. The clinical endpoint is urgency episodes. This satisfies the rule's explicit cohort-description alternative.
- **Perampanel, NCT00566462 — exclude.** The participants have idiopathic PD and perampanel is in an active arm, but the only registered outcomes are striatal/caudate/putamen imaging binding after levodopa challenge. No disease-relevant clinical effect is an objective or outcome. The separate perampanel extension records have clinical OFF-time outcomes and are judged independently.
- **Isradipine, NCT00753636 — exclude.** The objective expressly seeks “safety and tolerability of isradipine”; the primary measure is completion/tolerability. A motor UPDRS secondary measure is present, but the objective does not explicitly study the drug's clinical effect, so that measure does not independently satisfy the clinical gate.
- **Glycerol phenylbutyrate, NCT02046434 — exclude.** The objective is movement of alpha-synuclein from brain to bloodstream, and the detailed description expressly says “No effects on Parkinson symptoms are expected during this short trial.” Both PD and healthy cohorts receive the drug, but the study is biomarker-only.
- **UDCA, NCT02967250 — exclude.** A PD cohort receives ursodeoxycholic acid, but the specified aims concern brain ATP/MRS bioenergetics and drug concentrations. The registered primary outcome is “Change in ATP concentration,” without a qualifying clinical effect objective. The presence of healthy controls is not itself the reason for exclusion.
- **Dapagliflozin and sitagliptin, NCT06263673 — uncertain.** Eligibility accepts “Parkinson's disease or Lewy Body Dementia,” and each drug has a distinct active arm and neurological outcomes. The registry does not specify separate Parkinson and Lewy body dementia treatment cohorts with separately specified clinical outcomes. Under the frozen mixed-cohort clause, neither occurrence is included.
- **Capsaicin, NCT02183519 — exclude.** The study has a separately identifiable Parkinson cohort, but capsaicin elicits reflex cough during cough-function testing. The objective compares physiology of voluntary and reflex cough, not capsaicin's clinical treatment effect.

The healthy-volunteer interaction records for paracetamol, warfarin, repaglinide, rifampicin, rosuvastatin, caffeine, carbamazepine, midazolam, and clarithromycin were excluded even when the study's condition tag mentions Parkinson disease. Their eligibility or explicit objective describes healthy participants and PK or drug-interaction endpoints. Duplicate drug/trial occurrences, including the BF 2.649 dose arms, were adjudicated individually; no decision was inferred solely from a drug name or condition tag.

The source-path and verbatim-span check passed with `repurpose.strict_labels.validate_decisions`. This check confirms record coverage and quote integrity; the clinical interpretations above remain subject to independent review.
